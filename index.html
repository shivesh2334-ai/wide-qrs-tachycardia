The diagnostic criteria

Brugada sequence

For regular monomorphic wide-complex tachycardia: no RS in any chest lead → RS interval >100 ms in any chest lead → AV dissociation → morphology compatible with VT in V1/V2 and V6. A positive step favors VT; stop at the first positive step.

Only when every step is confidently negative does the algorithm favor SVT with aberrancy. Unknown findings leave the sequence incomplete. Pre-excitation, pacing, drug effects and baseline conduction disease can confound it.

Scope and limitations

  • No AI image classifier, probability estimate, validated clinical score or automated ECG measurement.
  • Rules depend on clinician-entered findings and cannot establish a definitive diagnosis.
  • Brugada criteria are limited to regular monomorphic WCT; missing information never becomes a negative finding.
  • No patient identifiers are requested. Uploaded images remain in browser memory and are cleared on refresh.
  • Drug dosing and individualized prescribing are outside this app’s scope. Use local resuscitation protocols and specialist review.
  • The article does not include a dedicated polymorphic VT/torsades tracing; the app explicitly identifies this image gap.
